The Real Problem

You didn't buy AI.
You bought more work.

Every company is different. Your tools, your team, your processes — none of it looks like anyone else's. That's exactly why this problem is still unsolved. The fragmentation is invisible from the inside — until someone maps it, prices it, and shows you what it's actually costing you every single day you leave it alone.

This isn't an AI problem. It's an integration problem. When your tools don't talk to each other, your people become the connectors — manually copying data, chasing updates, bridging gaps that should close themselves. Every closed deal, every new client, every escalated ticket triggers a chain of manual steps across systems that have no idea the others exist. The AI you bought made the noise louder. It didn't fix the wiring.

We come in, map exactly where your stack breaks, cut what's redundant, and wire the rest together so your business runs the way you always assumed it did.
