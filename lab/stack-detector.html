What this can and can't see

It reads the public-facing stack: CMS, hosting, analytics, chat widgets, marketing and AI tools that leave a footprint in the site's code.

It cannot see internal back-office tools — finance, HR, an internal CRM — that never touch the public site. The Detector complements the manual input in the Stack Analyzer; it does not replace it.

AI-app detection is the least mature area industry-wide. The fingerprint set is curated and growing, so it will miss things. Confidence is shown honestly and unmatched signals are surfaced rather than guessed.
