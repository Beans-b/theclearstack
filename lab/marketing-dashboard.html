Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Generating is free and there is no signup to start. We only ask for your email if you want your plan exported.

Do I need a marketing background?

No. Every module explains itself in plain language and the output is copy-paste ready.

What does it generate?

A positioning line, a week of social posts with image briefs, and SEO & AEO copy — page titles, meta descriptions, headlines, and FAQs — for any page of your website.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

It asks the right questions first, then returns structured, ready-to-use pieces — not a wall of chat text. You copy each piece to where it belongs.

Does it store my business data?

Your dashboard is saved in your own browser. We only keep an email address if you choose to share one.

Who built it?

The Clear Stack, an AI consolidation consultancy for mid-market companies. The dashboard is a free tool from our Lab.
