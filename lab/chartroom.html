Chartroom

Rather than learning how to prompt ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, or any other AI, Chartroom writes the prompt for you. It builds a self-verifying prompt that loops — checking and fixing its own work — for a smarter, more effective answer.

A SeaLegs instrument · The Clear Stack

  • It loops. Drafts, critiques, fixes, repeats — instead of guessing once.
  • It fact-checks. Backs every claim with a fact, a source, or your materials.
  • It knows when it's done. A success checklist tells it when to stop.
  • For builders: front-loads the acceptance criteria and forces a skeptical second pass — less hallucination, fewer wasted tokens.
